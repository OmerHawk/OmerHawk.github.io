## Presentation Topic

### Real-Time Telemetry & IoT Architecture in Formula 1

Modern motorsport relies heavily on high-speed data acquisition and real-time processing to optimize race strategies and vehicle performance. This presentation explores how IoT infrastructure, sensor networks, and edge computing operate together under extreme environmental conditions in Formula 1.

* **Embedded Sensor Networks & ECU Processing:** Over 300 high-precision onboard sensors continuously monitor critical variables such as tire temperatures, brake fluid pressure, dynamic G-forces, and engine RPM. The central Electronic Control Unit (ECU) aggregates and processes thousands of data points per second directly on the vehicle.
* **Low-Latency Wireless Transmission & Pit Wall Analytics:** At speeds exceeding 300 km/h, a single race generates nearly 1 Terabyte of telemetry data. This section details how high-frequency trackside antennas transmit this massive data stream with sub-millisecond latency to pit wall engineers and remote factory hubs for instantaneous strategy adjustments.
